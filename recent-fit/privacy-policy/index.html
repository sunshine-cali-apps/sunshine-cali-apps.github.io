Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This app uses Apple HealthKit to read and save workout data in order to provide workout logging and recent workout features.

Health Data

Health data accessed through Apple Health is used only for app functionality. The app does not sell, share, or transmit Health data to external servers.

Data Collection

This app does not require user accounts and does not collect personal information for advertising or marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or external tracking services.

User Control

Users can manage or revoke Apple Health permissions at any time in the Apple Health app settings.
