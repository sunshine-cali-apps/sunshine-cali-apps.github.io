Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my workouts stored?
Workouts are stored in Apple Health. The app only stores your recent workout preference in your phone, not in any third party server.

Why does the app need Health access?
The app uses Apple Health permissions to save workout data entered in the app to Apple Health.

Can I revoke Health permissions?
Yes. You can manage Health permissions anytime in the Apple Health app settings.
